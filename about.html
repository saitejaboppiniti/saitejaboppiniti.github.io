About Me

I'm a Senior Data Engineer by profession and an independent researcher by passion. By day, I build large-scale data systems that power enterprise decision-making. Outside work, I research how AI and machine learning can move healthcare from reactive to proactive.

On the engineering side, I'm currently a Senior Data Engineer at The Kroger Co., working on large-scale data infrastructure that powers enterprise decision-making. On the research side, I've published 33 papers and accumulated 500+ citations. My research is independent, done on my own time and separate from my day job.

One of the projects I'm most proud of is developing AI-embedded smart contact lenses that continuously monitor health metrics in real time — glucose levels, intraocular pressure, ocular health indicators. That work led to a published paper, international press coverage across three continents, and a registered UK design.
